Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11

Service provider: 程均. This policy describes the data practices of the current version of Nookimo.

Scope and contact

This policy covers local games, nearby multiplayer and user-requested support in the Nookimo iOS app. For privacy questions and data requests, contact sibiconstant1207@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

The app stores your nickname, a randomly generated player identifier, language and audio preferences, practice completion, some game progress, and information needed to resume nearby sessions. The random identifier is not your real name, but can identify the same player in nearby sessions. The current app has no registered accounts, developer-operated game servers, advertisements, third-party analytics SDKs, in-app purchases or subscriptions. These local records are not automatically uploaded to the developer.

Nearby multiplayer and permissions

Nearby features, including resuming unfinished sessions, discover rooms over a local network and exchange room and player identifiers, nicknames, admission and recovery credentials, actions, game state, scores and timing data. Creating a room advertises the host's nickname, game type, player count and room status to devices discovering rooms. The host's device manages the game and participants see the player information needed for play. Avoid real names, contact details and sensitive information in nicknames.

Local network permission is for discovering and connecting to devices, not accessing location, contacts, your camera or microphone. You can play locally without this permission and change it in iOS Settings. The system chooses an available local network route; Bluetooth-only connectivity is not promised. The current transport uses TCP without app-level TLS encryption. Play with people you know on a trusted network.

Local diagnostics and voluntary sharing

The app caches connection and runtime events locally. Raw records may include timestamps, system runtime timing, random node, room and session identifiers, and error details. Logs are rotated by size, up to approximately 512 KiB; they are not all automatically deleted after seven days. The developer does not automatically receive them.

Diagnostics offers a previewable summary of up to 100 permitted events from the last 500 raw records within the last seven days, with app and iOS versions. It filters out nicknames, raw identifiers, credentials and raw error details. A recipient receives what you send only after you confirm sharing, choose a recipient and complete sending. Temporary export files are removed when sharing ends or the page is released. Deleting the app does not remove copies others have received.

Email support and external services

If you email us, we receive your sender address, message and chosen attachments to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. The app only pre-fills app and system versions; it does not automatically attach diagnostics or send email. Send only information necessary to resolve your request.

Email is handled by your chosen provider and Google Gmail, which hosts the support mailbox. If support and legal pages are hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub records visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. Visiting webpages requires internet access. These services may process data outside your region and have their own privacy policies. Our support pages have no added advertising or analytics code. We do not sell personal information or use support information for cross-app tracking or targeted advertising.

Retention, deletion and choices

You can edit your nickname and preferences in the app. Game saves and recovery information are retained, overwritten or cleared as game flows require. iOS may clear diagnostic caches. Deleting the app removes local data; Offload App keeps documents and data. Some preferences may be included in system backups and restored, according to your device settings. Current game save and recovery credential files are excluded from system backups.

We retain support information only to handle your request and necessary follow-up, or longer where applicable law requires. Contact the email above to request access, correction or deletion of support information we actually hold, or exercise other applicable rights. We cannot remotely inspect or delete local data on your device or other participants' devices. There is no registered Nookimo account to delete.

Legal bases and applicable rights

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, support information is processed to respond to your request, pursue legitimate interests in keeping the app working, or comply with legal obligations. We obtain consent where legally required. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, data portability, withdraw consent, and complain to your local data protection authority. Withdrawal does not affect lawful processing based on consent before withdrawal. Making a privacy request does not affect local gameplay.

Minors and updates

Minors should use the app in accordance with local law and guardian requirements, avoiding unnecessary personal information in feedback. Contact us if a support message contains information about a minor that should not be retained. Nookimo is a general-audience casual game and is not offered as an App Store Kids Category product.

We will update the policy and date when practices change and provide notice or obtain consent where required by law. All language versions describe the same practices. Please report translation questions to the contact email.
