Cannot find a nearby room?

Install compatible Nookimo versions, enable Wi-Fi and allow local network access for Nookimo in iOS Settings. Try the same trusted Wi-Fi network; device isolation can prevent discovery. The host creates a room and approves admission. Members ready up, then the host starts the game.

Can I play without internet?

Local games and nearby play over an available local connection do not need internet. Webpages and sending email do. Local solo play does not require local network permission. Bluetooth-only connectivity is not promised.

Accounts and pricing

No account is needed. The current version is free, without ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Game points have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for cash or prizes.

Manage local data

Edit your nickname, language and audio preferences in the app. Delete the app to remove local data; Offload App keeps data. Manage system backups and information already sent separately. See the Privacy Policy for details.
